
#  Employee Wellness Analysis – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

##  Project Overview

Organizations increasingly recognize that employee well-being directly impacts productivity, retention, and workplace culture. Following a critical incident involving the loss of a key employee, **XYZ Technical Solutions** sought to better understand the mental health and wellness risks within their workforce.

This project performs a **comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on employee wellness data to identify patterns, risk indicators, and organizational gaps that may contribute to declining employee health. The analysis aims to support **data-driven wellness interventions** and informed HR decision-making.

---

##  Objectives

The key goals of this project are to:

* Understand factors influencing employee mental health and well-being
* Identify employees who **may require mental health treatment or intervention**
* Analyze the impact of workplace conditions such as workload, support systems, and company policies
* Provide actionable insights to design **targeted wellness programs**
* Improve employee productivity, engagement, and retention through informed interventions

---

##  Dataset Overview

The dataset captures a wide range of **mental health, workplace, and demographic attributes**, offering insights into how organizational environments affect employee wellness.

### Key Themes Covered:

* Mental health status and treatment needs
* Workplace stressors and work-life balance
* Employer support, benefits, and policies
* Employee openness and stigma around mental health

---

##  Dataset Features

The dataset includes the following attributes:

* **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Country, State
* **Employment Context:** Self-employed, Company Size, Remote Work, Tech Company
* **Mental Health Indicators:** Family History, Treatment Requirement, Work Interference
* **Workplace Support:** Benefits, Care Options, Wellness Programs, Employer Support
* **Organizational Culture:** Anonymity, Leave Policies, Stigma, Observed Consequences
* **Communication Comfort:** Willingness to discuss mental health with coworkers, supervisors, or employers
* **Additional Context:** Absenteeism trends and open-ended comments

##  Methodology

###  Data Understanding

* Explored dataset structure, variable meanings, and context
* Identified categorical vs numerical attributes
* Understood the organizational problem statement and analysis goals

###  Data Preprocessing

* Handled missing and inconsistent values
* Standardized categorical responses
* Treated outliers and noisy data
* Prepared clean, analysis-ready datasets

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Distribution analysis of mental health indicators
* Relationship analysis between workplace factors and treatment needs
* Visualization of trends across demographics and job environments
* Comparative analysis of employees with and without support systems

###  Insights & Interpretation

* Identified employee segments at higher mental health risk
* Highlighted organizational factors correlated with treatment needs
* Assessed effectiveness of employer wellness initiatives

---

## Key Insights

* Employees lacking access to mental health benefits show **significantly higher treatment needs**
* Poor work-life balance and high workload correlate strongly with work interference
* Stigma and fear of negative consequences reduce willingness to seek help
* Organizations with wellness programs and anonymity protections show **better employee outcomes**
